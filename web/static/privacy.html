Privacy policy

Plainspoken, effective 16 November 2026

Plainspoken is a language course app for Android and iOS, and a website at language.zach.ro. It has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and it collects no personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress (unit status, practice results, your review deck and your settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. On Android it is included in your device's own backup when Android backup is turned on, under your Google account and Google's terms. You can export your progress as text from the Progress page and delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

What the app downloads

The app downloads course content and recordings from language.zach.ro and repository.language.zach.ro, which are served by Cloudflare. Like any web server, these receive the standard information of each request, such as your IP address and device type, which Cloudflare processes to deliver the files and to protect the service. We do not combine it with anything else or use it to identify you.

The website

The website keeps your progress in your browser's local storage. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views; that service sets no cookies and does not track you across sites.

Children

Plainspoken is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date. Questions: hello@zach.ro.
